Ductless air conditioner repair finds why a mini-split isn't cooling or heating, whether that's a blocked drain, a dirty coil, a failed sensor, a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units, or low refrigerant. In Mississauga we repair ductless systems and heat pump mini-splits.

This page covers common symptoms, error codes and when a cleaning is enough.

What are common mini-split problems?

  • Water dripping from the indoor unit

  • Weak airflow or a musty smell

  • An error code or blinking light

  • The outdoor unit won't start

  • Cooling in summer but no heat in winter

What does an error code mean?

It points to a system: sensors, communication, the drain or the compressor. Call with the code and the make and model.

Book an in-home quote

Fill out the form, or call and we'll book it with you.

Is a cleaning enough?

Often, when the problem is airflow or a blocked drain. A technician can tell on the first visit.
